## Spaceship Platform Services
The CLI interacts directly with the official Spaceship domain and web services platform ([spaceship.com](https://spaceship.com)). Platform pricing is managed directly by Spaceship:
- **Domain Registrations & Renewals**: Standard registrar rates based on TLD (e.g., .com, .org, .io, .ai).
- **DNS Hosting & Management**: Free DNS record hosting included with registered domains.
- **Advanced DNSSEC & Custom Nameservers**: Supported according to domain tier.
- **Domain Transfers & Restorations**: Billed according to registry and transfer pricing schedules.
- **SellerHub Marketplace**: Commission and listing fees determined by Spaceship marketplace terms.
- **Hyperlift Hosting**: Web application deployments and hosting services billed according to active plans and resource usage.

For official domain pricing schedules, marketplace terms, and hosting rates, visit [https://spaceship.com](https://spaceship.com).
